Rest Solar Agent

A trilingual (EN / FR / ZH) AI support agent for a solar company in Cameroon. It answers stock and price questions and hands off to a human in one tap.

Key features

  • Rule-engine guardrails: never invents prices, adds 19.25% VAT automatically
  • Multi-country admin: Cameroon / Nigeria / Sudan / Mali
  • Live-agent takeover with a waiting queue and real-time chat
