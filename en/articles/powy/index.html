Powvy Habit: tasks, focus and everyday life

Powvy Habit is a personal growth PWA that challenges performative discipline. It brings tasks, workload, goals, focus and life records into one tool.

An idea does not have to become a task immediately

Today schedules tasks and displays workload, warning about overload without blocking it. The Growth Pool holds ideas outside the daily load until they are ready to become tasks. Users advance long-term goal progress manually.

Tools for focus and study

The focus module provides a Pomodoro timer and a record dashboard. Offline flashcards support decks, due reviews, and JSON and CSV import and export. Their simplified review intervals do not claim FSRS compatibility.

Local data and optional sync

The Life page contains nutrition, wallet and daily records. Data stays on the device by default. Optional account sync covers core tasks and flashcards; Life data remains local. The interface supports Chinese and English.
