Restar Solar: websites, support and product management

I developed a website and supporting tools for my family's business serving African markets. The project includes company websites, AI support, online support from staff, and product management and updates.

Ongoing maintenance behind the website

Visitors need product information and a way to ask questions; staff need to maintain that information. The project connects the public website with admin tools. Staff have started using the admin interface to update products.

AI and human support together

The project includes both AI support and online customer service from employees. Its scope extends beyond an automated chat window to the tools business staff use to participate in service and maintain content.
