A-Level: study plans grounded in practice records

A-Level Survival Bureau is a local-first study workspace for Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Economics and Computer Science. Its manga-inspired interface calls study sessions Hunts and plans Contracts, while the underlying system organizes learning around actual practice records.

No records means unknown

Practice attempts are stored as events in the browser database. The system combines FSRS retrievability, accuracy and evidence volume to represent learning state. Topics without attempts are marked UNSCOUTED rather than assigned a zero score.

Planning around available time

The planner uses the free time declared by the user and explains its study allocations. Past-paper grade comparisons require the relevant boundaries from the user. Estimates in the app are not official predicted grades.

Content scope and backups

Content packs are scoped to exam boards and years; unverified subject content stays locked. Learning event records support JSON import and export. Web data stays in the current browser, without automatic cross-device sync.
